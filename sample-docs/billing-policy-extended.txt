Department: Billing & Financial Services
Title: Standard Policy – General Billing and Insurance Verification

1. Policy Statement
All patients receiving inpatient or outpatient services must have a clear financial arrangement established prior to service completion or discharge.

2. Guidelines
* Insurance Collection: Financial representatives will verify coverage, primary benefits, and copayment responsibilities upon admission or intake.
* Patient Responsibility: Copayments, deductibles, and non-covered services are due at the time of service or prior to final account settlement.
* Pending Authorization Handling: If insurance authorization is pending, the patient must sign a conditional financial responsibility agreement before final release from the nursing unit.
* Statement Issuance: Itemized statements are dispatched within seven business days following final account adjudication. Payment plans may be established through the billing office upon request.
