Department: Nursing
Title: Standard Operating Procedure – Patient Discharge Procedure

1. Purpose & Scope
This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) outlines the mandatory steps required for discharging inpatients from nursing care units.

2. Procedure Steps
* Medical Clearance: Confirm that the attending physician has written and signed the formal discharge order in the Electronic Health Record (EHR).
* Discharge Education: Provide the patient or primary caregiver with verbal and written instructions covering home medications, follow-up appointments, activity restrictions, and emergency contact details.
* Administrative Verification: Check the patient's billing status prior to discharge. If insurance authorization is pending, the patient must sign a conditional financial responsibility agreement before final release from the nursing unit.
* Transportation & Safe Departure: Escort the patient via wheelchair or assisted transport to the primary hospital entrance once all documentation and belongings are verified.
