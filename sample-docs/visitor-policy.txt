Department: General / Front Desk
Title: Standard Policy – Guest Visitation Guidelines

1. Overview
To maintain a quiet, safe, and healing environment for patients, all visitors must abide by established hospital visitation hours and safety rules.

2. Guidelines
* Visiting Hours: General visiting hours are restricted to 8:00 AM through 8:00 PM daily. Intensive care units may enforce custom visiting schedules.
* Visitor Limits: A maximum of two guests are permitted at a patient’s bedside at any given time. Guests under the age of 14 must be accompanied by an adult.
* Check-In Requirements: All visitors must check in at the main entrance reception desk, present valid government-issued identification, and display a temporary visitor badge at all times.
* Restricted Access: Visitors displaying symptoms of contagious illness will be denied entry to patient care areas.
